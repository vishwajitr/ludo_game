# Ludo – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 Nov 2026

The Ludo game itself does not collect personal information. It shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device information to deliver them, as described below.

## About this policy

This privacy policy applies to the Ludo mobile game for Android (the "App"), developed and published by Vishwajit ("I", "me", or "the Developer"). It explains what information the App handles and how. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

## Information the App collects

The App does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts or photos, and it does not require an account or sign-in.

The game works offline. The game data it keeps is stored only on your device:

* Game progress: saved games so you can continue where you left off
* Game history: the results of finished games, shown on the on-device leaderboard
* Settings: sound, music, vibration, animation speed, theme, and game rule choices
* Player names: any names you type for players in a match

This game data never leaves your device, and it is excluded from cloud backups. The Developer cannot see or access it.

## Advertising

The App shows banner ads from Google AdMob on its menu screens (never during a game). To show ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

* Device identifiers, such as the advertising ID
* Approximate location, derived from your IP address
* Device and app information, such as device model, OS version and app version
* Ad interactions, such as ads shown and tapped, and diagnostic data

In the EEA, the UK and other regions where it is required, the App asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Google's use of this data is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Permissions and third parties

The App uses internet access, network state and the advertising ID permission only to load ads. It requests no other sensitive permissions. Vibration is used only for haptic feedback during play.

Apart from Google AdMob (including Google's consent SDK), the App contains no analytics or tracking tools, no in-app purchases and no other third-party SDKs that collect data. The Developer does not sell or receive any of your information.

The App is a free game of chance and skill for entertainment only. It involves no real money, betting, or prizes.

## Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences (rated up to PG). The Developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child's information has somehow been shared with the Developer, please get in touch using the contact details below and it will be deleted.

## Deleting your data

All game data lives on your device. To delete it, clear the App's storage in your phone's Settings (Apps → Ludo → Storage → Clear storage), or uninstall the App.

## Security

Game data is never sent off your device and is protected by your device's own security. Ad requests are sent to Google over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

## Changes to this policy

If the App changes how it handles data, for example by adding online play, this policy will be updated before that version is released. The "Last updated" date at the top will show when it last changed.
