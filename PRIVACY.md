# Ludo – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 Nov 2026

Ludo does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.

## About this policy

This privacy policy applies to the Ludo mobile game for Android (the "App"), developed and published by Vishwajit ("I", "me", or "the Developer"). It explains what information the App handles and how. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

## Information the App collects

The App does not collect any personal information. It does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers, and it does not require an account or sign-in.

The App works fully offline. The only data it keeps is stored locally on your device:

* Game progress: saved games so you can continue where you left off
* Game history: the results of finished games, shown on the on-device leaderboard
* Settings: sound, music, vibration, animation speed, theme, and game rule choices
* Player names: any names you type for players in a match

This data never leaves your device. The Developer cannot see or access it.

## Permissions and third parties

The App does not request internet access or any sensitive device permissions. Vibration is used only for haptic feedback during play.

The App contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, no in-app purchases, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. No information is sold, shared, or transferred to anyone.

The App is a free game of chance and skill for entertainment only. It involves no real money, betting, or prizes.

## Children's privacy

The App is suitable for players of all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child's information has somehow been shared with the Developer, please get in touch using the contact details below and it will be deleted.

## Deleting your data

All App data lives on your device. To delete it, clear the App's storage in your phone's Settings (Apps → Ludo → Storage → Clear storage), or uninstall the App.

## Security

Because no data is sent off your device, it is protected by your device's own security. The App never transmits your information over the internet.

## Changes to this policy

If the App changes how it handles data, for example by adding ads or online play, this policy will be updated before that version is released. The "Last updated" date at the top will show when it last changed.
